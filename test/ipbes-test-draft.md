# Report of the workshop on "nature-based solutions" and the IPBES work programme

## Introduction

The workshop was organised by the Intergovernmental Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services (IPBES) and held in Bonn, Germany, from March 3-5, 2026. It was the second such workshop - following the first in 2025.[^1]

Participants from 32 countries attended, including experts from Turkey, the Czech Republic and Vietnam. 45% of participants were women.

## Main findings

### Participation

Participants included:

(a) representatives of IPBES members;
(b) observers, including:
  (i) indigenous peoples; and
  (ii) local communities; and
(c) experts nominated by the Secretariat.

### Discussions

Each participant presented his/her views on the BBA and the SPM of the nexus assessment (e.g. chapters 2 and 3). The Chairman noted that decision-makers and policy makers must urgently adopt groundbreaking measures.

> The Platform should be "policy-relevant but not policy-prescriptive".
