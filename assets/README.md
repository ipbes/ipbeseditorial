# IPBES brand assets

These assets were extracted from `authorities/2021 IPBES Visual Guidelines.pdf`, the IPBES Logo and Visual Identity Guidelines. They are for web and design work, which is handled separately from the editorial skill. The masthead logos used in official documents are in `skills/ipbes-editorial/assets/masthead/`.

## Folders

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `logo/` | The IPBES logo in SVG (preferred), PDF and PNG. **Original** and **medium teal** versions are for light backgrounds. **Mono** is for single or limited colour on light backgrounds. **Reversed** (on teal, orange or grey) is for dark full-colour backgrounds. **Whiteout** (on teal, orange or grey) is for dark backgrounds in single or limited colour. The `clear-space` versions show the minimum clear space. |
| `motifs/` | The four visual-identity motifs (fingerprint, ecosystems, species, genetics) in SVG, PDF and PNG |
| `reference-pages/` | Page images of the guidelines (pages 01–10), for checking against the original |

## Logo rules (guidelines page 3)

- Use the logo with or without the strapline "Science and Policy for People and Nature".
- Do not distort or dismantle the logo, and do not place it on a background that reduces its impact.
- Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the ascender of the "b". For the strapline version, measure the clear space from the baseline of the strapline. The clear space is already built into the artboards of the logo files.
- Never redraw or generate the logo; use these files.

## Typography (page 4)

- **Helvetica Neue:** Light, Light Italic, Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold Italic.
- If Helvetica Neue is not available, use another version of Helvetica, or Arial.
- Official UN-format documents (mastheads, Plenary documents) use **Times New Roman** instead (see `skills/ipbes-editorial/references/report-structure.md`).
