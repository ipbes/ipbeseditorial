# IPBES names and terms

These names and terms are taken from the Plenary reports IPBES-1 to IPBES-12 (see `evidence.md`) and from `authorities/IPBES_Assessment_Abbreviations.pdf`. Where the two disagree, the reports win and the difference is noted.

## 1. The Platform

- **Full name:** Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services (IPBES).
  - Give it in full at first mention in the body text.
  - **Never shorten it** to "Intergovernmental Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services". Every word of the name is required, including "Science-Policy".
  - Never write "Science Policy" (missing hyphen) or "Science-policy" (lower case "p").
  - In Word, a non-breaking hyphen (Ctrl+Shift+-) in "Science‑Policy" keeps the name from breaking across lines in titles.
- **After first mention**, use "IPBES" or "the Platform".
  - Use "IPBES" in narrative.
  - Use "the Platform" in formulaic and agreed text: "members of the Platform", "the work programme of the Platform".
- **No possessive on the acronym.** Write "the IPBES secretariat" and "the mandate of IPBES". Do not write "IPBES's" or "IPBES' mandate".
- **Strapline:** "Science and Policy for People and Nature" (from the logo). Use it only as part of the logo; do not invent other taglines.

**Sessions.**

- In narrative, write "the twelfth session of the Plenary" or "at its twelfth session".
- Use "IPBES-12" as shorthand only where it is already used (headings, tables, informal text).
- Meetings within a session take figures: "at its 2nd meeting".
- Number sessions in words: "first" to "twelfth", "thirteenth"…

- **Session column.** The "Approved" column gives the session at which the Plenary approved the summary for policymakers. Check it against the decision before citing it.

## 5. Document symbols

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| IPBES/12/1 | provisional agenda of the twelfth session |
| IPBES/12/1/Add.1 | addendum (here, annotations and organization of work) |
| IPBES/12/6 | pre-session working document |
| IPBES/12/INF/3/Rev.1 | information document, first revision |
| IPBES/12/L.3 | limited-distribution draft, usually a draft decision |
| IPBES/12/11 | report of the session |
| decision IPBES-12/1 | Plenary decision. Use a hyphen between "IPBES" and the session number, and a slash before the decision number. |

- Symbols never break across lines. In Word, use a non-breaking hyphen in "IPBES‑12/1".
- Ranges: write "decisions IPBES-12/1–IPBES-12/3", with an en dash and the symbol repeated in full.
- Cite documents in parentheses after the title: "the note by the secretariat on improving the effectiveness of the Platform (IPBES/10/9)".
- Cite earlier decisions in running text without italics: "in decision IPBES-11/1".
